# Chess Break — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

Chess Break does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No account.** You never sign in.
- **No network access.** The extension works fully offline. The chess engine (Stockfish), the puzzles and all images ship inside the extension. Nothing is downloaded at runtime.
- **Local storage only.** Your settings, puzzle progress, streaks and saved games stay in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) on this device. They are never synced or uploaded.
- **You control your data.** Settings → Data lets you export it to a file, import it back, or delete everything at any time. Uninstalling the extension also removes it.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.**
- **No access to websites.** Chess Break doesn't read pages you visit and never touches games on Chess.com, Lichess or any other site.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `storage` | To save your progress, streak, settings and the game or puzzle in progress on your device |
| `sidePanel` | To let longer games stay open beside the page while you browse |
